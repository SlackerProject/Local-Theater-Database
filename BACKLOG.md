## Awards

- **Credit awards to the company too.** When an award goes to a person (or a production), the company that produced the show should get credit for it as well: it should appear on the company's page ("Awards for our productions") and in any company totals. Each award entry already names its production, and the production names its company, so this can be shown without asking contributors for anything extra. Do it when the award system gets its full pass.

## Before public launch

- **Merge duplicate people.** A moderator tool to combine two person pages that are the same person, moving all credits to one. (Christian Roulleau has two pages waiting for this.)
- **Custom domain, then an email service.** Supabase's built-in email only sends a few messages an hour, and only to the project team, so public sign-up confirmations need a proper email service once the domain is set up. Email also unlocks notifications by email and telling claimants their claim was approved or rejected.
- **Claiming follow-ups.** Let a claimant withdraw a pending claim; tell them in the app when it's verified or rejected.
- **A short privacy page** explaining that email addresses are stored only for sign-in.
- **Clean up unused uploads.** A poster uploaded on the Add a production form is stored immediately; abandoned drafts and rejected submissions leave files behind.
- **Watch storage and bandwidth.** The free plan includes 1 GB of storage (about 3,000 images) and 5 GB of bandwidth a month (Supabase dashboard > Project Settings > Usage).
- **Time zone per venue.** Times are treated as Massachusetts time everywhere.

## Adding productions: making it quicker

- Done: the cast and crew lists match the edit page; the location fills in with the company's usual venue; a known show lists its roles.
- Ideas:
  - Remember the last company and venue a contributor used and offer them first.
  - "Copy from a past production": start from an earlier production of the same company (venue, crew who return every season).
  - Paste a whole program page (cast and crew together) and sort it into the two lists.
  - Suggest the year from the dates entered, and the performance times the company usually uses.
